# Password Reset Procedure

Applies to: company network and email accounts.

## Self-service password reset
1. Open the self-service portal at https://password.company.example from any device.
2. Enter your company email address and select "Forgot password".
3. Verify your identity with your registered mobile number or authenticator app. A six-digit code is sent to you.
4. Choose a new password and confirm it.
5. Wait about five minutes, then sign in again on your laptop. If you are off the office network, connect to the VPN using the new password.

## Password rules
- Minimum 12 characters.
- Must include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol.
- Cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 90 days. You receive an email reminder 14 days before expiry.

## If you cannot use self-service
If you have not registered a mobile number or lost your phone, contact the IT Service Desk. They verify your identity with your manager and reset your password. Service Desk staff will never ask you to tell them your password.

## After resetting
Update the saved password on your phone email app and on any mapped network drives, otherwise old saved passwords can cause repeated account lockouts.
