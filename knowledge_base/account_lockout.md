# Account Lockout Guide

Applies to: company Windows and email accounts.

## Why accounts get locked
An account is locked automatically after 5 failed sign-in attempts within 15 minutes. This protects you against password guessing. Common causes are a mistyped password, an old saved password on a phone or tablet, and a mapped network drive using an outdated password.

## What to do
1. Wait 30 minutes. The account unlocks automatically after the lockout period.
2. To unlock immediately, use the self-service portal at https://password.company.example and choose "Unlock my account". You must verify your identity using the authenticator app.
3. If self-service does not work, contact the IT Service Desk. The Identity and Access team can unlock the account after verifying who you are.

## Prevent repeated lockouts
- Remove your old password from your phone's email and Wi-Fi settings.
- Sign out and sign in again on any other computers or remote sessions where you were logged in.
- Check Windows Credential Manager for saved company passwords and update them.

## Suspicious lockouts
If your account locks while you did nothing, or you receive unexpected MFA prompts, do not approve them. Change your password and report it to the Security team immediately because someone may be attempting to guess your credentials.

## Access to systems after unlock
If you can sign in but still cannot open an application or shared folder, your permissions may be missing. Submit an access request through the service catalog and name your manager as the approver.
